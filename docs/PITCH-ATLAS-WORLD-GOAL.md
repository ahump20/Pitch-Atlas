# Pitch Atlas — a world of pitching knowledge

Direction received 2026-09-05: build Pitch Atlas as a compelling, coherent world of baseball knowledge and discovery. Preserve the strong study/compare flow; judge every detail by how it deepens the larger product world, emotional pull, and learning journey.

This is the next product goal. The verified Archive Within Reach implementation remains the foundation; its completed evidence is not being relabeled as proof that this larger experience is finished.

## Working goal

Make Pitch Atlas a place people enter through a grip or a question and keep exploring because the craft connects: a hold leads to a feel cue, a variation, a practitioner, a historical experiment, and a real conversation. Preserve the heritage and evolving art of pitching while helping visitors find something they can understand, inspect, compare, and carry forward.

The product world includes baseball and the existing softball wing. Its coherence comes from connected knowledge, original hands, authored editorial choices, and traceable human perspectives. The existing cards, material depth, motion, and controls are the means of expressing that world.

This applies the current [Northstar](NORTHSTAR.md); it does not change a Core commitment.

## Governing product criteria

1. **An inviting front door.** A new visitor understands what lives here, why it matters, and where their curiosity can lead. A pitcher who already knows what they need still reaches the index or grip quickly.
2. **Meaningful connections.** Related grips, variants, practitioners, history, and lessons connect through documented relationships. Links explain why the next destination matters. Mere proximity in a grid is not a learning relationship.
3. **A memorable point of view.** Real hands, preserved experiments, disagreements with clear attribution, and the transmission of craft supply the emotional pull. There is no invented community activity or borrowed celebrity authority.
4. **A learning journey with continuity.** Study and comparison remain strong. A visitor can leave them to follow context and return with their selected pitches and place intact.
5. **A living archive.** Discussion and contribution explain how a visitor's firsthand finding can join the record, within existing rights and moderation capabilities. Empty discussions remain honest and useful.
6. **A coherent experience across surfaces.** Home, Index, Grips, Craftsmen, Lost Pitches, Learn, softball, and the existing native tabs feel like connected parts of one product. Composition and rhythm can differ according to the content's purpose.
7. **Detail earns its place.** A visual or interaction change must improve understanding, orientation, emotional connection, or the next useful action. Motion should make a relationship or change of context clearer. Still frames and reduced-motion reading must hold up independently.

## Acceptance evidence

The next delivery must demonstrate three coherent paths using real existing content:

- A pitcher finds a grip, studies its reference and variation, compares a related pitch, and returns without losing context.
- A curious reader enters through a historical experiment or practitioner, understands a documented connection, follows a relevant lesson or specimen, and can explain what they discovered.
- A returning learner reaches the real discussion for the pitch or topic they studied and understands how to contribute a firsthand observation with its appropriate source and rights context.

Capture before/after compositions and continuous journey recordings. Review the experience at desktop, 390px mobile, short landscape, and native accessibility text sizes. Verify reduced motion, missing media, no-WebGL reading, browser/navigation restoration, and the existing offline reference behavior supported by available environments. Run the relevant web and native checks. Do not count isolated polished components or passing unit tests as proof of the whole journey.

Reviewable source changes, a verified web preview, native build/test and available simulator evidence, and an updated evidence index complete this bounded implementation delivery. The qualitative judgment must explicitly address coherence, emotional pull, and learning continuity.
